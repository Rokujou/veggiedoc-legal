VeggieDoc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

This Privacy Policy explains how VeggieDoc ("the App", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android. We are committed to collecting the minimum personal data necessary to provide the service.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller)

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

CS Analytics LLC
A United States limited liability company, operated from Türkiye.
Contact: veggiedoc@csanalytics.app

VeggieDoc is a mobile app that diagnoses plant and leaf diseases from photos you take, using artificial intelligence, and then provides organic and chemical treatment plans and care guides. It is intended for home and hobby gardeners and is not directed at children.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account email — when you create an account.
  • Authentication provider ID — an identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google when you use those sign-in options.
  • Anonymous guest sessions — if you use the App without creating an account, we use an anonymous session identifier so the App can function.
  • Plant records you enter — plant names, notes, and care progress that you choose to save.
  • Photos you submit for diagnosis — see Section 3.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active trial or subscription (managed through the app stores and RevenueCat; we do not receive your payment card data).

We design for minimal personal data ("PII") by default and do not ask for more than we need.

3. How Photos Are Processed — Third-Party AI Disclosure

Important: When you request a diagnosis, the photo you submit is transmitted to a third-party AI provider, Plant.id (operated by Kindwise s.r.o.), for analysis. This means your photo leaves our systems and is processed by that provider in order to identify the plant and any possible disease.

  • What is sent: the image you choose to submit for diagnosis.
  • Why: to perform the AI analysis that produces your diagnosis and treatment suggestions.
  • Model training: we do not train any AI models ourselves, and your photos are not used to train our models.
  • Retention: diagnosis photos are processed transiently. We do not retain diagnosis photos beyond what is needed to display your result. Plant.id's own processing is governed by Kindwise s.r.o.'s privacy terms.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core service: diagnosing plants and showing treatment plans and care guides.
  • To create and manage your account and anonymous sessions.
  • To save and display the plant records you enter.
  • To manage your subscription, free trial, and access entitlements.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the service.
  • To respond to your support requests.

7. International Data Transfers

We operate from Türkiye, our company is established in the United States, and our database, authentication, and storage are hosted by Supabase in the Asia-Pacific region. Plant.id (Kindwise s.r.o.) is based in the European Union. As a result, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your account data is deleted.
  • Diagnosis photos are processed transiently and are not retained beyond what is needed to show you the result.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR (EU) and the KVKK (Türkiye — Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format;
  • Object to certain processing.

How to exercise your rights

  • Delete your account directly in the App: Settings → Delete account.
  • Export your data directly in the App: Settings → Export data.
  • Or email us at veggiedoc@csanalytics.app and we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children

VeggieDoc is intended for home and hobby gardeners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including access controls and row-level security on our database so that you can only access your own records. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. Affiliate Links

The App may show affiliate product suggestions (for example, gardening supplies). If you tap such a link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These purchases happen on external third-party websites that have their own privacy policies and terms; we are not responsible for those sites.

13. The AI Disclaimer

VeggieDoc provides AI-generated suggestions for informational purposes only and is NOT a substitute for a professional agronomist or plant-health expert. Treatment suggestions, including chemical products, are used at your own risk. Always read product labels and follow all safety and toxicity warnings. We make no warranty of accuracy.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
